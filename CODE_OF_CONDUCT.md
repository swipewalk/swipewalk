# Code of conduct

Swipewalk follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good intent, and focus on what is best for the people
who use the apps being tested. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not
accepted, including about disability.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/swipewalk/swipewalk/security/advisories/new)
(mark it as a conduct report), or contact a maintainer listed on the
[Swipewalk organization page](https://github.com/swipewalk). Reports are kept confidential.
Maintainers will respond as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
